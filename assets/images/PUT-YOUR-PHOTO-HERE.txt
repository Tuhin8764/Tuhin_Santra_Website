HOW TO ADD YOUR PROFILE PHOTO
==============================
Save your photo as exactly:   profile.jpg
in this folder (assets/images/). That's it — it's used in two places
automatically once the file exists:
  1. A small circular photo in the top-left navbar (click it to view
     full-screen).
  2. A full-bleed photo behind the "Hi, I'm Tuhin Santra" hero text, faded
     into the dark background with a gradient (no code changes needed).

TIP FOR THE BEST LOOK
----------------------
The hero photo blends into the dark background using a gradient overlay,
but it blends most naturally if the photo itself already has a soft,
blurred, or gradient-style background (a portrait/headshot style photo)
rather than a busy photo background or a hard-edged studio backdrop.
Free tools like remove.bg (to cut out the background) or Canva can help
create this style if you don't already have a photo like that.

Delete this text file once you've added profile.jpg — it's just a
placeholder note and isn't linked from the site.
